Licence text: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

`theme_fast.wav` is not a second recording. `tools/process_audio.py` builds
it from `espionage.ogg` by resampling the timeline to 1.4× speed (pitch
rises with the speed) and crossfading the loop join.
